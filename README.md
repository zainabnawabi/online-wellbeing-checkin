# How Do You Feel Online? A 12-Question Check-In

A short, free self-reflection questionnaire about life online, built for the course *Advanced Theories in Cyberpsychology*.

**Try it:** https://zainabnawabi.github.io/online-wellbeing-checkin/questionnaire.html

## What is it?
The check-in asks 12 simple questions about phones, social media, games and the internet. It is based on humanistic psychology, especially the ideas of Carl Rogers (being yourself and feeling accepted) and Abraham Maslow (safety, belonging and growth).

## What does it measure?
Your answers are grouped into four areas, each scored out of 3 (12 in total):
- **Being yourself online:** do you show your real self?
- **Kindness to others online:** do you understand others, and do you feel accepted?
- **Technology helping you grow:** does your online time support your goals and values?
- **Feeling safe and in control:** do you feel in charge of your privacy and security?

Your score appears only after the last question, with a plain-language explanation.

## Privacy
Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

## Important note
This is a teaching and reflection tool, not a validated psychological test. It cannot diagnose anything.

## Run it yourself
Download `questionnaire.html` and open it in any browser. No installation is needed.
