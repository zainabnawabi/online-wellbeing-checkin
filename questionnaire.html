WHAT WAS THIS CHECK-IN?

It was a short self-reflection based on two well-known psychologists, Carl Rogers and Abraham Maslow. They believed people are healthiest when they feel accepted, can be themselves, and keep growing. This check-in asks whether your online life supports those things.

It is not a medical test and cannot diagnose anything. Use it to think about your habits.

HOW THE SCORE WORKS

There were 12 questions, and each is worth up to 1 point. "Completely true" gives 1 point, "Not true at all" gives 0, and the answers in between give part of a point. For the three negative statements (like "Social media makes me feel worse"), it works the other way round, so a low answer earns more. The best possible score is 12.
